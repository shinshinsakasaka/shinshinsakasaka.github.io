## About Me

<img class="profile-picture" src="profile.jpg">

Hi! I am a PhD student in Informatics at Indiana University Bloomington (IUB), where I am advised by Dr. [Haewoon Kwak](https://luddy.indiana.edu/contact/profile/index.html?Haewoon_Kwak) and Dr. [Jisun An](https://luddy.indiana.edu/contact/profile/index.html?Jisun_An) at [Soda Lab](https://soda-labo.github.io/). We are a member of the Center for [Complex Networks and Systems Research (CNetS)](https://cnets.indiana.edu/). Currently, I also closely work with Dr. [Goren Gordon](https://luddy.indiana.edu/contact/profile/index.html?Goren_Gordon) (IUB) and Dr. [Katharina Reinecke](https://www.cs.washington.edu/people/faculty/katharina-reinecke/) (University of Washington). I have worked as a research intern at Aalto University. I attended the [CMMRS 2022](https://cmmrs2022.mpi-sws.org/).

You can download my CV [here](CV.pdf)
